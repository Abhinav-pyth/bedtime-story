## 🎮 How to See Hindi Stories (3 Easy Ways)

### Method 1: Home Page (Easiest)
1. Look at the **top right** of the home page
2. You'll see a button: **🇮🇳 हिंदी**
3. Click it → Instantly see all 20 Hindi stories!

### Method 2: Stories Page
1. Click the **Stories** tab (📚 icon)
2. At the top, click the language toggle button
3. Switch between 🇬🇧 English and 🇮🇳 हिंदी
4. All stories update instantly

### Method 3: Settings Page
1. Click **Settings** (⚙️ icon)
2. Find the **Language / भाषा** section
3. Click **🇮🇳 हिंदी**
4. Your choice is saved automatically
